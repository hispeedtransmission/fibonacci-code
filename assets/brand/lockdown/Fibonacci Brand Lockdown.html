Three F systems.

The mark has to survive as a one-color silhouette, resolve at 16 pixels, and carry one scarce orange verification event. These are structural alternatives, not color swaps.

A / Slab

The current production primary. Fast silhouette, severe geometry, strongest small-size performance. Less closely tied to the modular mark you remembered.
